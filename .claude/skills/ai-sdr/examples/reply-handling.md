# Reply Handling (worked examples, ICP + email)

How to respond to interested replies from ICP buyers. Classify intent, then draft a grounded reply.
All claims trace to `knowledge/offer.md`. **Deliver the give first; the give earns the meeting.**
Pricing appears **only** on a direct pricing ask.

---
### 1. Meeting/demo accept — *"Yes, let's talk. How's next week?"*
Deliver value + make scheduling effortless.
> Great, Maya. Before we even meet — here's the signal play our SDR AI built for Driftwave, plus the 3 sample emails it drafted for your top targets [attached]. Grab whatever's useful. For the call, here's my calendar: [link] — or just tell me a couple of windows and I'll send an invite. I'll come ready to show how this runs on your stack in ~5 weeks.

---
### 2. Info request — *"Send it over."* / *"Sure, share it."*
This is the win condition for a value-first CTA. Deliver immediately, then a light next step.
> Here you go, Maya — the personalized signal play for Driftwave, built off your hiring and tech-stack signals [attached]. If it's useful, the same engine works those accounts automatically across email + LinkedIn and books the meetings — Memgraph got to $2.7M pipeline / 60 BANT deals in 90 days this way. Want me to run a quick pipeline-gap analysis next — how many meetings you need to hit target this quarter?

---
### 3. Pricing ask — *"What does this cost?"*
Only here do we discuss price. Anchor on Scale, note opt-out + included engineer, frame vs a hire.
> Fair question, Maya. Plans run from $3,500/mo (Starter); most Series-A teams start on **Scale at $5,500/mo**, which adds website de-anonymization and technographic signals. Every plan includes a forward-deployed GTM engineer who builds it with you, the email + LinkedIn infrastructure, and CRM integration — and it's a **3-month opt-out**, not an annual lock-in. For context, that's well under a single fully-loaded SDR, for 3–5x the meetings. Want me to map which tier fits Driftwave's motion?

---
### 4. Objection — "We already have SDRs." / "AI feels spammy."
Reframe to augmentation + the guardrails, then re-offer value.
> Totally — this isn't a replacement, Maya. Your reps stop spending the day on research, writing, and CRM and spend it in meetings and closing; the worker just covers the accounts they can't get to (3–5x output, same team). Every email is researched and reviewed against guardrails before it sends, and it lands in the primary inbox, isolated from your domain. Happy to prove it — want the teardown of Driftwave's current outbound with 3 things I'd change?

---
### 5. Referral — *"Talk to our Head of Sales, Priya."*
Thank, ask for the warm intro, carry the give to the new contact.
> Appreciate it, Maya — Priya sounds like the right owner. Want to intro us, or should I reach out and mention you pointed me her way? Either way I'll bring the signal play our SDR AI built for Driftwave so she's got something useful from minute one.
